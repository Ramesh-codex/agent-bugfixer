# Customer names garbled after CSV import

After importing today's orders CSV, several customer names are coming out
garbled in our system. Accented letters are either missing entirely or
replaced with strange characters.

For example, a customer named "José Martínez" shows up in our system as
something like "Jos Martnez" — the accented characters just disappear.

This only affects customers with non-English characters in their name. We
have a lot of international customers, so this is causing real problems on
packing slips and shipping labels.

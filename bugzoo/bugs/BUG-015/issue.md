# CA tax off by a cent on a $10.00 order

Finance ran their monthly audit and found a discrepancy: a $10.00 order
shipped to California was charged $0.72 in sales tax. Finance's reference
tax table says a $10.00 CA order should be charged $0.73.

It's a one-cent difference, but it showed up on more than one order during
the audit, always in the same direction (we charge less than we should),
and always on amounts that land on a particular kind of boundary — finance
couldn't tell us exactly which amounts, just that it isn't random.
